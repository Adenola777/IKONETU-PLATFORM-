Trust, Privacy and Accountability

A workshop for data, privacy and product teams on who answers for a decision, and how personal data is handled along the way.

Discuss this programme View the outline agenda

At a glance

Audience
Data protection, product, legal and risk teams
Format
Facilitated workshop, in person or online
Duration
Half day or full day, agreed in the proposal
Participation
Data mapping, scenario discussion and responsibility mapping
Possible outputs
Data flow map, decision record, safeguards list with owners
Preparation
Briefing call and a short description of the product or process
Follow-up
As specified in the proposal
Group size
Agreed at the briefing call

Questions explored

The group works through these questions using a product or process your organisation runs.

  1. 1What personal data does this product or process use, and why?
  2. 2Who is accountable for each decision the system makes or supports?
  3. 3How will we explain a decision to the person it affects?
  4. 4How can someone challenge an outcome, and who responds?

Outline agenda

Half-day version
0:00 – 0:20 Opening and purpose. The product or process in scope, and what today should produce.
0:20 – 0:50 Accountability and data protection in practice. UK GDPR principles, controller and processor roles, and where AI changes the picture.
0:50 – 1:40 Mapping the data. Groups trace personal data through the product or process.
1:40 – 1:55 Break
1:55 – 2:45 Responsibility and explanation. Groups name owners and agree how decisions are explained and challenged.
2:45 – 3:15 Safeguards check. Current safeguards compared with what the group found, and gaps ranked.
3:15 – 3:30 Decisions and next steps. What was agreed, what is still open, and who follows up.

This programme is a good fit if

  • You are launching or changing a product that uses personal data
  • Product and privacy teams need a shared view of who is responsible
  • You want a written output, not just a discussion
