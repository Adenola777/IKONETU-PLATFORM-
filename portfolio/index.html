About

I am a barrister and solicitor by training. I now run technology companies in Leicester, where governance, contracts and data protection are my responsibility. My advisory work and the sessions I lead draw on that experience.

I write on AI governance for Brainz Magazine as an Executive Contributor. I also volunteer as an Enterprise Adviser with The Careers & Enterprise Company.

Biography for organisers

Adenola Adegbesan is a barrister and solicitor, called to the Bar in 2016, and an Associate of the Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators of Nigeria (ICSAN). He founded InspireCraft Global Ltd and co-founded IkonetU Technology Ltd, both based in Leicester, and is responsible for governance, contracts and data protection in both. He writes on AI governance as an Executive Contributor to Brainz Magazine. In 2026 he spoke at the World AI Assembly and Tech Derby, and was nominated for Innovation in AI and Technology at the Black Excellence in Governance Awards. He was a keynote panellist at the B2B Growth Expo in Milton Keynes in 2025 and 2026.
