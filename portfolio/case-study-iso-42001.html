ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system readiness

A gap assessment and roadmap for an organisation building an AI management system to ISO/IEC 42001, the international standard for managing AI responsibly.

Client sector
Organisation using AI across several product lines
My role
Gap assessment and readiness adviser
Worked with

1

The situation

Much of the documentation the standard expects already existed, but it was scattered across teams and not organised against the standard. The organisation needed to know which gaps were real and what it still had to build.

2

What I did

  1. 1Clause-by-clause gap analysis. Current practice compared with each requirement, separating real gaps from existing documents that had not yet been organised against the standard.
  2. 2AI impact assessment process. A repeatable assessment of the impact on individuals and on society more widely, run before each new AI use case goes live.
  3. 3Management system documents. The policies, procedures and role definitions needed to run the system day to day.
  4. 4Internal audit programme. An audit checklist and schedule to keep the system working after certification.

3

How I approached it

I built the documents around how the organisation already develops and deploys AI, so that staff would keep using them after the audit.

4

Where this work applies

  • Organisations pursuing ISO/IEC 42001 for procurement or competitive reasons
  • AI teams that need a repeatable impact assessment
  • Compliance teams setting up AI governance from scratch
