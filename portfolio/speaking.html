On stage

Talk topics

Each talk is adapted to the event and audience. Lengths are a guide.

For boards, risk, legal and data leaders

Governing AI Before It Fails

Most AI failures trace back to governance decisions that could have been made early. How to set up accountability, data controls and incident readiness before a system goes live.

Audiences leave knowing

  • Who owns an AI decision, and how to record it
  • How a small team can build data protection in from the start
  • The questions a board should ask before sign-off

Given at the World AI Assembly 2026 · 30 to 45 minutes

For SME owners and business networks

AI That Pays Its Way

A practical session for owners who want results they can measure, drawn from building an AI tool for online retailers.

Audiences leave knowing

  • Where small firms lose money that AI can recover
  • How to test a tool for 30 days before paying for it
  • The legal basics: customer data, consent and contracts

30 to 45 minutes, or as a panel contribution

For product, privacy and risk teams

Trust Is a Business Decision

Trust is decided long before a product reaches its users: what information is collected, how decisions are explained, and how people can challenge an outcome.

Audiences leave knowing

  • How to spot privacy and fairness questions early in development
  • The difference between having a policy and running a safeguard that works
  • How to design complaints and escalation in from the start

30 to 45 minutes

Short biography

Adenola Adegbesan is a barrister and solicitor who runs technology companies in Leicester, where he is responsible for governance, contracts and data protection. He is an Associate of the Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators of Nigeria (ICSAN) and advises organisations on AI governance and regulation. In 2026 he spoke at the World AI Assembly and was nominated for Innovation in AI and Technology at the Black Excellence in Governance Awards.
