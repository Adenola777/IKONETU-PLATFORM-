From brief to record

  1. Before · 1

    Understand

    A briefing call to agree the question, who will be in the room and what the session must produce.

  2. Before · 2

    Design

    A session plan, scenarios and worksheets you can review, plus any agreed pre-reading.

  3. On the day · 3

    Facilitate

    Small-group work and structured discussion, with every voice heard and time kept.

  4. After · 4

    Capture

    A written record of decisions, open questions and owners, sent as agreed in the proposal.

What participants do

Exercises I use include:

Scenario discussion

Groups work through a realistic case and decide what they would do, and why.

Stakeholder mapping

Who is affected by a decision, who should be consulted and who carries the risk.

Responsibility mapping

A worksheet that names an owner, a reviewer and an escalation route for each decision.

Structured decision log

A running record of what was decided, what is still open, and who will follow up.
