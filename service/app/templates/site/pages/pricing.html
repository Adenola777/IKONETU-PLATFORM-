Pricing

Start free, and pay for verification.

Founders and investors each have two plans. Free covers the pitch, search and introductions, and Premium adds verification.

Premium is not on sale yet. It opens once IkonetU can take payments, and nobody is charged today.

For founders

Founder plans

Free

Founder Free

$0

It is free for student founders aged 18 or over, in any country.

  • You publish a founder profile and a one-minute pitch with captions.
  • You add evidence, which investors see as Founder submitted.
  • Your statements about yourself count towards your IkonetU Score.
  • You see your IkonetU Score and your next three steps.
  • You post progress updates, which show investors your recent activity.
  • Investors find you in search, ask for introductions and talk with you.
Create founder profile
Premium

Founder Premium

$5 a month

It will be billed monthly in US dollars.

Founder Premium includes everything in Founder Free, and adds:

  • IkonetU verifies each piece of your evidence against its source.
  • Verified evidence counts towards your IkonetU Score.
  • Investors see that your evidence is verified, and when.
  • Your profile appears when investors filter for verified evidence.

Premium opens once payments are ready.

For investors

Investor plans

Free

Investor Free

$0

It is free for investors aged 18 or over, once an IkonetU admin approves the account.

  • An IkonetU admin checks your account before it opens.
  • You search founders by sector, stage, country and recent activity.
  • You watch each founder's pitch and see the profile details and the ask.
  • You see the status of each piece of evidence.
  • You ask for introductions and talk with the founders who accept.
Create an investor account
Premium

Investor Premium

$29 a month

It will be billed monthly in US dollars.

Investor Premium includes everything in Investor Free, and adds:

  • You see what IkonetU compared for each verified piece of evidence, and when.
  • You read the founder's summary of each piece of evidence.
  • You see the full profiles of verified founders, with their updates.
  • You filter for verified revenue and registry records.

Premium opens once payments are ready.

Questions about pricing

When can I buy Premium?

Premium opens once IkonetU can take payments. Nobody is charged until they choose Premium and agree to pay for it.

How will billing work?

Premium will be billed monthly in US dollars. Before you pay, IkonetU will show you the price, when it renews and how to cancel.

What does verification check?

IkonetU compares each piece of evidence with its source, such as bank statements, a registry record or a customer reference, and records what it compared and when.

The terms set out how fees work, and the Trust and safety page explains what each evidence status means.
