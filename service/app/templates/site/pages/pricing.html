Questions about pricing

When can I buy Premium?

Premium goes on sale in December 2026, together with face verification and anti-money-laundering checks. Nobody pays until they choose Premium and agree to the price.

How will billing work?

Premium bills monthly in US dollars. Before you pay, you see the price, the renewal date and how to cancel.

What does verification check?

IkonetU compares each piece of evidence with its source, such as a bank statement, a registry record or a customer reference. It records what it compared and when.

The terms set out how fees work, and the Trust and safety page explains what each evidence status means.
